Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in North Las Vegas

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Minor issues rarely stay small for long. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a few miserable nights.
