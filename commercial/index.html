Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air out all shift long, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Carson City HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your operating hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and customers notice the drafts at the door and the stuffy dining room right away. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by written reports and service logs after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases require.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we install during off-hours, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable in the meantime.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Carson City HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Carson City and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, get the building comfortable again, and document the entire call for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
