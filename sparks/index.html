Two situations call for quick action from you before our technician gets there. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or produces a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and call us. Those few minutes can save you from a much larger repair.

Keeping Your Sparks HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet the same part, ignored through a full season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast the moment something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Comfort Challenges Sparks Homeowners Face
